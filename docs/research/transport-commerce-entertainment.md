# Transport, commerce and entertainment discovery

Checked on 2026-07-23 using public pages only.

## Result

- 11 candidate records: 6 shopping/delivery, 2 transport/travel and 3
  entertainment.
- 6 records are directly listed in the public partner data on the official
  Zalo OA homepage.
- The remaining public `zalo.me` pages are reachable and expose a title and
  description, but their verification badge is not present in the public HTML.
- All candidates remain `active=false`.

## Important gaps

- A reachable `zalo.me` page is not sufficient evidence of a verified badge.
  Badge status must be checked in the Zalo app by a reviewer.
- CGV has no approved logo reference in this dataset. Other logo URLs are
  references only and must not be hotlinked or copied without approval.
- Vietnam Airlines, Vexere, CGV and Galaxy Play need an independent official
  cross-link or owner confirmation before activation.
- GO! currently appears under the historical BigC asset in the Zalo partner
  list; the card asset must be refreshed before production use.
- Do not promise booking or payment inside an OA unless that exact flow has
  been tested. Several records are currently information/chat entry points.
